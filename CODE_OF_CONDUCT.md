# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Welcome participants with different backgrounds and experience levels. Discuss technical decisions and evidence without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, or disclosure of another person's private information.

## Project safety

This project performs destructive disk operations. Do not encourage unsafe testing, conceal deployment risk, or publish disk serials, credentials, internal logs, or other sensitive deployment details. Use private vulnerability reporting for security concerns.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate these standards, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for repeated or serious violations.

Report conduct concerns privately to [vartaxe@outlook.com](mailto:vartaxe@outlook.com). Maintainers will review reports in good faith and protect reporter privacy where reasonably possible.
